_Last updated at August 27, 2026_

This Privacy Notice explains how Your personal data is handled when You register for or attend the webinar **"CRA Is Live: What Software Teams Have to Do Now"**, held online on 17 September 2026 (the "Webinar"). The Webinar is jointly organised by JobRunr and Cresco.

This notice supplements, and does not replace, the [JobRunr Privacy Policy]({{< ref "pages/privacy" >}}) and the [Cresco Privacy Policy](https://crescolaw.com/privacy-policy/).

## Who We Are

The Webinar is organised by two companies acting as **joint controllers** within the meaning of Article 26 GDPR for the personal data described in this notice:

- **JobRunr BV** ("JobRunr"), with registered office at Gallicstraat 68, 3300 Tienen, Belgium. Contact: nicholas@jobrunr.io
- **Cresco Advocaten BV** ("Cresco"), registered under enterprise number 0548.916.070, with registered office at Lange Kievitstraat 118-120, 2018 Antwerp, Belgium. Contact: info@crescolaw.com

## What Data We Collect

When You register for or attend the Webinar, We may collect:

- The registration details You share through the LinkedIn event registration form, such as Your name, job title, company and the information visible on Your LinkedIn profile
- Your email address, if You provide it to Us
- Questions and comments You submit before, during or after the Webinar, including replies to messages in which We ask which topics You would like covered
- Attendance information, such as whether You registered and whether You joined the live stream

## Why We Use Your Data

Both JobRunr and Cresco may use this data to:

- Organise, run and improve the Webinar, including collecting Your questions in advance so they can be answered during the session
- Send You practical information about the Webinar, such as reminders, the replay and the slides
- Follow up with You after the Webinar about the topics covered and about related products, services and events offered by JobRunr or by Cresco

The legal basis for this processing is Our legitimate interest in organising the Webinar You registered for and in following up with the professionals who chose to attend it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where consent is required for specific communications, We will ask for it. You can object to follow-up communications or unsubscribe at any time.

## How the Data Is Shared Between Us

The essence of Our joint controller arrangement is as follows:

- JobRunr manages the LinkedIn event page and receives the registration data from LinkedIn
- JobRunr shares the registration data with Cresco
- **Both JobRunr and Cresco may use the registration and attendance data for the purposes listed above, including their own follow-up communications**
- Each party is responsible for its own subsequent use of the data under its own privacy policy

Irrespective of this arrangement, You can exercise Your rights against, and direct any question to, either of Us.

## LinkedIn

Registration for the Webinar runs through LinkedIn Events. LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company processes Your data as an independent controller under its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy). This notice only covers what JobRunr and Cresco do with the data they receive.

## How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep Webinar registration and attendance data for as long as needed for the purposes above and no longer than 24 months after the Webinar, unless You engage further with Us (for example by becoming a customer or client) or a longer retention period is required by law.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete and receive a copy of Your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can exercise these rights with either organiser:

- JobRunr: nicholas@jobrunr.io
- Cresco: info@crescolaw.com

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit, [www.dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be)).

## Changes to This Notice

If We update this notice, We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

## Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to nicholas@jobrunr.io or info@crescolaw.com.
